Cookie Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

In short

Shadow AI sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. The only thing stored in your browser is what is strictly needed to keep you signed in and to make the extension work.

1. What we store in your browser

WhereWhatWhyHow long
Website — local storageYour sign-in session (access and refresh tokens)Keeps you signed in to the dashboard. Strictly necessary.Until you sign out
Website — session storageA marker for which dashboard tab to return to after Google sign-inReturns you to the right page. Strictly necessary.Removed immediately after use
Extension storageYour extension session, your workspace’s detection settings, and any event metadata waiting to be sentLets the extension detect and report while you work, including briefly offline. Strictly necessary.Until you sign out of the extension or uninstall it

None of this is used to track you across sites or to build a profile.

2. Third-party requests

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and Fontshare, and open-source code from jsDelivr and cdnjs. Loading these files means your browser contacts those providers, which can see your IP address, as with any web request. They do not set cookies through our pages. [For EU launch, consider self-hosting these files so no third party is contacted.]

3. Do we need your consent?

No consent banner is shown because we only store what is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for. Under the EU ePrivacy rules and the UK PECR this kind of storage is exempt from consent. If we ever add analytics or any non-essential storage, we will ask for your consent first.

4. Your choices

You can clear this storage at any time by signing out, clearing your browser’s site data, or uninstalling the extension — you will simply need to sign in again.
