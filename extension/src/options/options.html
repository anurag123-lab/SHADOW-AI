What this extension sends

When something is flagged, one row is recorded containing: the category of what was found (for example "payment card number"), the AI tool's hostname, your department, the action you chose, and an anonymous ID.

It does not send your message, your file, the matched text, your name, or your email address. The database this writes to has no column capable of storing message content — that is enforced by the database schema itself, not by a promise in this page.

Detection, redaction previews and every decision happen locally in your browser. The extension works with no network connection; events queue and send later.
